- [Download the PDF report](../../releases/latest/download/Simplicio_MCP_Token_Reuse_Evidence_Report_PT_EN.pdf)
- [Read the evidence page](https://simpleti.com.br/simplicio/evidence/)

The report distinguishes context reuse from financial savings. Its measured
example reports approximately 98.01% cache reuse, while estimated input-cost
savings depend on cache pricing and total output. Cache-hit percentage is not
itself a claim of equal financial savings.
